## Using presets in the website

Open Create → Spell. Use **Browse spell presets and categories** to search all catalog spells by family or result support. **Use as template** opens an independent editable copy. The **Spell behavior** selector chooses shared hit/save/healing/condition rules; target shapes, amounts, and scaling stay in their existing fields. Keep saved recipe preserves combined and area recipes. Required fields and incompatible combinations are checked before saving.

## Rules and limits

Behavior recipes are versioned and validated before saving or enabling results. Unknown recipes, incompatible hit/save rules, missing damage/healing/condition data, and invalid area/projectile settings cannot enable automation. Existing saved results and areas retain legacy compatibility. Class lists, preparation, eligibility, casting ability, and resource spending remain separate.

The runtime uses recipe data for shared projectile counts/rolls, conditions, restoration, target restrictions, and recurring-area triggers. Spell-specific settings live in data recipes rather than spell-name branches. Copying a catalog spell retains its recipe with an independent ID.

The manual families describe the work still needed; they do not run speculative effects. Existing catalog targeting gaps are listed explicitly below and are not silently corrected by categorization.
