## Multi-round casting workflow

Implemented in `combat/longCasting.js`. A saved attempt includes identity, spell definition snapshot, selected resource/source, required/completed rounds, last round/turn, scope and status. Starting/continuing spends one Action in the same transaction that saves progress. Repeating a turn cannot advance twice, including with Action Surge. Temporary concentration prevents automatic completion after interruption. Completion revalidates the latest actor/source/resource and the final turn, spends the resource once, releases temporary concentration or starts the spell's own concentration, and only then runs the existing confirmation/VFX path. Pending concentration saves block progress; cancellation spends no spell resource. Characters and monsters share this reducer. Supported fixed minute/hour combat casts are enabled; exploration, rituals, ambiguous casting times and unsupported targeting remain manual.
